### **IdentificationNicotine USP** is identified as a clear, colorless to pale yellow liquid or a fine white crystalline powder with a characteristic odor. It is highly potent and requires controlled handling.

- **Chemical Name:** (S)-3-(1-Methyl-2-pyrrolidinyl) pyridine
- **CAS Number:** 54-11-5
- **Molecular Formula:** C₁₀H₁₄N₂
- **Molecular Weight:** 162.23 g/mol
- **Appearance:** Clear liquid or white crystalline powder
- **Odor:** Strong, characteristic tobacco-like odor
