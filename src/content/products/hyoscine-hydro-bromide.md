**[Hyoscine Hydro Bromide](/)** is a muscarinic antagonist which is derived from Solanaceae. Due to its anticholinergic properties, it works by blocking acetylcholine receptors. This compound is used for the treatment of nausea & vomiting caused due to motion sickness. It is known to prevent connection between vomiting center in brain and nerves of vestibule. **Hyoscine Hydro Bromide** can be administered by oral, rectal, or intravenous routes.

**Features:**

- It is an alkaloid
- Excreted by kidney and feces
- Can also treat excessive salivation
