**Artemether IP** is a **high-quality pharmaceutical-grade antispasmodic agent** used to relieve **smooth muscle spasms** in the gastrointestinal, urinary, and biliary tracts. It is commonly prescribed to **treat irritable bowel syndrome (IBS), renal colic, and painful cramps** by relaxing the muscles of the digestive system.

It is available in **tablet, injectable, and oral liquid formulations**, providing **rapid and long-lasting relief**.
