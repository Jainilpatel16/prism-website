**[Hyoscyamine Hydro Bromide](/)** is medication which is prescribed for the treatment to provide relief from various gastrointestinal disorders. It is known to have chemical formula C17H24BrNO3 and molar mass of 370.287 g/mol. this compound is also found to be effective against symptoms of Parkinson’s disease. **Hyoscyamine Hydro Bromide** works by inhibiting the action of acetylcholine due to its anticholinergic property. It has the ability to donate 2 and accept 4 hydrogen bonds.

## Features:

- Has melting point of 152 degree Celsius
- It is a tropane alkaloid
- Acts as an antagonist to muscarinic acetylcholine receptor
