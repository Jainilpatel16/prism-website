**[Nicotine](/)**is a natural alkyloid , found in the tobacco plant that is a major component of cigarettes and is used therapeutically to help with smoking cessation.

**Identification**

Molecular Formula   :     C10H14N2

Cas No.                       :     54-11-5

**Regulatory Documents**

EDMF                         :

Batch Size                 :     500 Kgs.

**Specifications**

Appearance   :     Colorless or brownish viscous liquid, volatile, hygroscopic.

**Solubility**

Soluble in water, Miscible with anhydrous ethanol

Specific Optical Rotation   :     -140° to -152°C at 20°C

Water content                     :     Not more than 0.5 %

**Assay**

Nicotine   :     99.0 % to 101.0 % (Anhydrous basis)
