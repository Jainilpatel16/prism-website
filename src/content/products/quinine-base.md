**Quinine Base** is a **natural alkaloid** derived from the bark of the **Cinchona tree**, known for its **antimalarial and medicinal properties**. It serves as a **crucial pharmaceutical ingredient** in the development of drugs used to treat **malaria, cardiac arrhythmias, and muscle cramps**. [**Quinine Base**](/api_bulk_drugs/quinine-base/) has been widely recognized for centuries as an effective treatment, making it a valuable component in modern medicine.

### **Identification**

- **Chemical Name**: Quinine Base
- **Molecular Formula**: C20H24N2O2
- **Molecular Weight**: 324.42 g/mol
- **CAS Number**: 130-95-0
- **Appearance**: White to off-white crystalline powder
- **Odor**: Odorless
- **Solubility**: Freely soluble in alcohol and chloroform, slightly soluble in water
