## Gain an understanding of API Bulk Drugs and Their Position in Pharma

## What are Active Pharmaceutical Ingredients (APIs)?

[**Active pharmaceutical ingredients**](/products/) (APIs) are the biologically active pharmaceutical material in medications that produce the intended therapeutic effect. They are produced by strict manufacturing, quality testing, and regulatory oversight to make sure they are safe and effective.

The strength and success of any drug rely in large part on the purity, stability, and performance of the API. Consequently, the requirement for a reliable **API bulk drug manufacturer** has never been more pertinent.

**The Increasing Significance of API Bulk Drug Production**
