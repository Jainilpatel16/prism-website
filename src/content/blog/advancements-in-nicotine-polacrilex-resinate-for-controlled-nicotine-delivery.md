## **Understanding the Nicotine Polacrilex Resinate:**

Nicotine polacrilex resinate is essentially the formed complex of nicotine and ion-exchange resin. The formed resin allows for the controlled release of nicotine when exposed to saliva, making it suitable for oral dosage form development like lozenges and gums.

Free nicotine gets easily absorbed by the body, causing drastic rises in plasma concentration, but the nicotine polacrilex API helps overcome this through controlled delivery of nicotine, enabling a gradual reduction in the body’s need to fulfill the craving. This system is an ideal delivery mechanism for the smoking cessation products, and hence formulation companies prefer it over free nicotine, due to its:

Uniform dosage,

Better stability and reduced volatility as compared to free nicotine,

Enhanced compliance by the patient.
