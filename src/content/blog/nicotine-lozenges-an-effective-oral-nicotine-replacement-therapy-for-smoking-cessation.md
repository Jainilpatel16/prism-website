## **FAQs :**

What are nicotine lozenges?

Nicotine lozenges are orally dissolving products that deliver controlled amounts of nicotine to help reduce smoking cravings.

How do nicotine lozenges work?

They dissolve slowly in the mouth, allowing nicotine to be absorbed through the oral lining for gradual craving relief.

What are the key benefits of nicotine lozenges?

They offer controlled nicotine release, precise dosing, convenience, and a smoke-free alternative to cigarettes.

Are nicotine lozenges pharmaceutical products?

Yes, they are formulated using pharmaceutical-grade nicotine and produced under regulated quality standards.

How are nicotine lozenges different from patches or gum?

Lozenges provide oral absorption without chewing or skin application, offering consistent and discreet nicotine delivery.

Why is API quality important in nicotine lozenges?

High-quality APIs ensure product stability, accurate dosing, and regulatory compliance.

Are nicotine lozenges used globally?

Yes, they are widely used in smoking cessation programs across international pharmaceutical markets.
