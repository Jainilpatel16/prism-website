## Understanding APIs for IBS Formulations

An Active Pharmaceutical Ingredient, or API, is the active ingredient in a pharmaceutical product that offers its primary pharmacological activity. During preparation, APIs are used with other ingredients to create various medication dosages, such as tablets, injections, or capsules.

The formulation of the gastrointestinal product determines the choice of API as it is used in a formulation, and formulation and API selection are influenced by factors such as the therapeutic use, dosage form, formulation criteria, relevant pharmacopoeia standards, target market, and Regulatory authorities.

An API supplier for a pharmaceutical company would not only have to be considered in terms of availability when sourcing an API for IBS as it would also need to meet stringent product specifications, analytical method validation, consistent production and suitable documentation.

## **Why API Quality Matters**

API quality is an essential consideration when developing and manufacturing pharmaceutical formulations. The material should meet predefined specifications established for its intended application.

Important quality parameters may include:

- Identity
- Assay
- Purity
- Related substances
- Residual solvents
- Water or moisture content
- Physical characteristics
- Other product-specific parameters

The exact requirements vary according to the API, applicable pharmacopoeial standards, customer specifications, and destination market.

A quality-focused manufacturing system helps ensure that production processes remain controlled and that finished API batches are appropriately evaluated before release.

## Batch-to-Batch Consistency

Good Manufacturing Practices can guide production systems Pharmaceutical quality systems, oriented around good manufacturing practices, focus on aspects such as production controls, quality control, documentation, laboratory testing, equipment, personnel, hygiene and traceability.
