## What is Nicotine Sulphate?

**Nicotine sulphate** is essentially a salt form of nicotine, created by combining nicotine base with sulfuric acid. It appears as a clear to light brownish liquid and is water-soluble, making it perfect for formulations that require easy dispersion.

**Key Characteristics:Chemical Formula:** (C10H14N2)•H2SO4

Form: Liquid or solid (powder)

Usage: Pharmaceutical formulations, research, and pest control

Purity: Pharmaceutical-grade ≥99%

As a high-purity [**active pharmaceutical ingredient**](/products/), nicotine sulphate is mainly utilized in the production of nicotine replacement therapies (NRTs) like gums, lozenges, pouches, and transdermal patches.

### Applications of Nicotine Sulphate

1. **Nicotine Replacement Therapies (NRTs)**

Nicotine sulphate is the key ingredient in:

– Nicotine chewing gums

– Nicotine lozenges

– Nicotine strips

– Oral and transdermal patches

Thanks to its water solubility, it boosts absorption and bioavailability, making these products more effective.

2. **Smoking Cessation Aids**

As nicotine plays a crucial role in harm-reduction strategies, nicotine sulphate is found in lower-dose, slow-release products that replicate the experience of smoking without the harmful effects of combustion.

3. **Botanical Research and Insecticides**

Beyond the pharmaceutical realm, nicotine sulphate is utilized in botanical insecticides because of its strong neurotoxic effects on pests, offering an eco-friendly alternative to synthetic chemicals.
