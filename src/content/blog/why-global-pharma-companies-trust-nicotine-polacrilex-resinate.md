## What is Nicotine Polacrilex Resinate?

**Definition and Structure**

Nicotine polacrilex resinate is a nicotine-resin complex crafted for controlled release. In this formulation, nicotine is attached to an ion-exchange resin, allowing it to be released gradually when it interacts with saliva. This controlled release ensures a steady supply of nicotine, helping to manage withdrawal symptoms and curb cravings.

**Why Use Nicotine Polacrilex Resinate?**

When compared to free-base nicotine, nicotine polacrilex resinate provides a smoother and more gradual absorption, minimizing the chances of sudden spikes and side effects. It’s specifically designed for use in NRT products like chewing gums and lozenges.

One of the most popular uses for nicotine polacrilex resinate is in [**nicotine chewing gum**](/nicotine/). As people chew, nicotine is gradually released and absorbed through the lining of the mouth, giving them quick relief from cravings without the harmful substances found in cigarettes.

**Lozenges**

Nicotine polacrilex resinate is also commonly found in lozenges, which function similarly to gum but cater to those who prefer not to chew.

**Novel NRT Forms**
