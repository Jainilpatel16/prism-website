India’s pharmaceutical sector has solidified its place in the supply chain and has Indian API manufacturers supplying to both domestic and global pharmaceutical players. APIs, or [**Active Pharmaceutical Ingredients**](/products/), which make medicines act as drugs, are critical components in medicine formulation and given rising demands for reliable pharmaceutical ingredients; Indian manufacturers have been ramping up their capabilities to cater to the ever-diverse needs of consumers and regulators.

Growth isn’t being defined by merely providing competitively priced APIs. Today’s global buyers, look for a variety of qualitative aspects like quality, continuity in supply, technical expertise, regulatory know-how and business professionalism which Indian API manufacturers are now addressing, through manufacturing facility upgrades, investment into quality systems and product development, robust documentation and developing and nurturing long-term relationships with their customers around the world.

## **India's Growing Role in API Manufacturing**

This diverse industrial capability in the manufacturing ecosystem for Pharmaceuticals in India stretches from raw materials through to formulations and into specialty ingredients for the same in addition to other specialized chemical ingredients for drug production. The presence of an industry with this broad manufacturing base means that there is a ready supply of technical staff with knowledge and expertise; a robust production infrastructure; an array of quality controlled analytically capable facilities; and a range of global sourcing and sales capabilities. There is clearly increasing emphasis among Indian producers towards greater specialization of inputs, including greater effort in ensuring capabilities for more specialist as well as generic as well as well-understood **pharmaceutical raw materials** for which well-understood and more difficult-to control manufacturing processes are a must. These are factors that the world of pharmaceutical production needs to leverage – the global pharmaceutical production world can and must learn that, beyond the large list of generically manufactured commodities – there is an opportunity to access all these ingredients through knowledgeable, organized and globally reliable suppliers.

## **Investment in Modern Manufacturing**

The quality expectation by Indian pharmaceutical customers is to produce API under controlled and reproducible environment. Many [**Indian API manufacturers**](/) are now enhancing their production infrastructure and quality testing capability to fulfill the expectation.

To achieve this, modern manufacturing activities such as enhanced process monitoring, enhanced resource utilization of equipment, controlled production environment and enhanced analytical test can be proposed. There can be a process optimization as well to improve reproducibility and resource efficiency for the manufacturer.

A manufacturing capability has also to be evaluated and given importance by an API buyer along with the quality system, trained manpower, documentation and maintenance of consistent production quality.

## **Quality Is at the Center of Global Expansion**

Quality is perhaps the single most significant reason why international companies source Active pharmaceutical ingredients (APIs) from India. They cannot afford to work simply based on the promise of availability of stock or the competitiveness of pricing. Instead, they require assurance that the stock supplied them regularly meets prescribed standards.

This quality assured API production takes a number of linked process steps for.

This includes managing raw material controls. The laboratory analytical control that must exist for checking the purity and quantity of each API batch that leaves the manufacturing facility is an essential aspect. A significant quality control and assurance process is employed, as also for documentation practices and batch release activities. A good many criteria would typically include 

- Identification of the substance.
- Assay (value that measures chemical content).
- Limits of impurities.
- Presence of related substances (chemically related compounds).
- Certain physical and chemical properties.
- Uniformity from batch to batch of manufacture.
- Adequate analytical procedures for product assessment.
- Correct conditions of storage.
- Traceability of an API batch throughout the chain.
- Documentation of all aspects for each API batch.

Obviously, the specifications to be adhered to by each company supplying its API can be quite differ­ent depend­ing on whether the API to be purchased has any spec­ecial features it requires based on the application in which it will used and customer specifications of whether the API supplied complies with existing relevant official (like British Pharmacopoeia or International Pharmacopoeia) quality standards or not.

## **Challenges Facing Indian API Manufacturers**

Though India ranks very well today in the industry, expansion to international markets has its share of challenges. International regulations keep changing, and international clients demand more and more transparency in quality.

Manufacturers also have to consider the fluctuating availability of raw materials, logistics bottlenecks, environmental pressures, cost of production, and global competition from other pharmaceutical manufacturing countries.
