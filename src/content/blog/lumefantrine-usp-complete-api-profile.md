## **What is Lumefantrine USP?**

An active pharmaceutical ingredient (API), Lumefantrine USP, forms the key ingredient within fixed dose combinations, which are utilized in the management of uncomplicated Malaria. Because Lumefantrine has a long half-life it increases efficacy in combination therapies thus, reduces risk of resistance or relapse of malaria.

#### FAQs:

What is Lumefantrine USP?

 Lumefantrine USP is a pharmaceutical active ingredient used in combination antimalarial medicines. It meets United States Pharmacopeia (USP) standards for purity, potency, and safety, making it suitable for use in high-quality malaria treatment formulations.

How does Lumefantrine work in malaria treatment?

 Lumefantrine works by interfering with the malaria parasite’s ability to detoxify heme inside infected red blood cells. This disruption leads to the accumulation of toxic substances that kill the parasite, helping clear the infection effectively.

Why is Lumefantrine commonly used in combination therapies?

Lumefantrine is usually combined with artemisinin derivatives in fixed-dose therapies. This combination improves treatment effectiveness, reduces the risk of drug resistance, and enhances the overall success rate of malaria treatment.

What quality parameters are important for Lumefantrine USP?

Key quality parameters include assay content, related substances (impurities), loss on drying, residual solvents, heavy metal limits, and particle size distribution. These specifications ensure the API meets pharmacopeial standards and is safe for pharmaceutical formulations.

What pharmaceutical applications use Lumefantrine raw material?

Lumefantrine raw material is primarily used in the manufacturing of antimalarial combination tablets. It is also supplied for government malaria control programs, public health initiatives, and generic pharmaceutical drug production.

What manufacturing standards must Lumefantrine API follow?

 Lumefantrine API must be produced in GMP-compliant facilities using controlled synthesis, purification, and quality testing processes. Analytical techniques such as HPLC, GC, IR spectroscopy, and UV-Visible testing are commonly used to confirm quality and purity.
