Palliative care aims to achieve quality of life among terminally ill patients or patients diagnosed with life-threatening illnesses. Symptom management such as management of pain, nausea, vomiting and respiratory distress is paramount to achieve comfort and dignity in patient management. One of the most widely used compounds in this regard is hyoscine hydro bromide, a popular anticholinergic drug. The [**hyoscine hydro bromide**](/api_bulk_drugs/hyoscine-hydro-bromide/) API is a reliable agent which helps in the control of uncomfortable symptoms, thereby playing a crucial role in current palliative care.

## **About Hyoscine Hydrobromide**

The hyoscine hydro bromide is an anticholinergic drug which is effective in blocking acetylcholine receptors in the body thereby decreasing the amount of muscle activity or secretions. The anticholinergic API is widely used in the management of smooth muscle spasm, motion sickness and respiratory secretions. In palliative care, hyoscine hydro bromide API is extensively used to alleviate discomfort that affects the patient’s quality of life. Its rapid action and effectiveness makes it a suitable choice for most medical providers.

## **How the Mechanism of Action relieves symptoms**

The therapeutic mechanism of hyoscine hydro bromide works by stopping nerve signals from initiating muscular contraction or glandular secretions. This will have the effect of reducing the following symptoms:

– Control of nausea and vomiting; this is related to brain signals, such as in motion sickness

– Control of excessive secretions in the chest (the ‘death rattle’ in palliative care)

– Control of spasms in smooth muscles such as in the stomach or the urinary tract
