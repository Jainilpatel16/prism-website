## **What is Artemether?**

Artemether is a semi-synthetic derivative of artemisinin, a compound derived from the sweet wormwood plant (Artemisia annua). The artemether API is commonly employed in the management of malaria, specifically in P. Falciparum infections which are the most potent type of malaria parasite.

As a key artemether raw material it can be incorporated in both oral and injectable preparations. The speed at which it is able to target malaria parasites has positioned it as a vital component in combination treatments such as artemisinin-based combination therapies (ACTs), recommended by world health organizations.

## **How artemether is effective for Malaria treatment**

Artemether’s success against malaria is attributed to its distinct mechanism of action. Once it enters the body, it generates active molecules that attack and destroy malaria parasites at an early stage of the infection process. ArtemetherAPI works to rapidly decrease the concentration of parasites in the bloodstream and as such it is an effective malaria treatment API.
